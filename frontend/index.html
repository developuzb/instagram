Webhook Sozlamalari

Serverdan olinmoqda...

Sozlash qadamlari:

  1. developers.facebook.com/apps ga kiring
  2. App → Webhooks bo'limiga o'ting
  3. Instagram ni tanlang → Subscribe
  4. Yuqoridagi Webhook URL va Verify Token ni kiriting
  5. comments va messages fieldlarni obuna qiling
  6. "Verify and Save" tugmasini bosing
